◒ STORY TOOLS

Don't just remember the story. Organize it.

Furina's story tools keep track of where a scene is, what changed, what happened before it, and where you might want to return later.

Scene State, recaps, timelines, snapshots, bookmarks, chapters and Story Bibles are stored on Furina's side of the experience. They organize your roleplay without rewriting Clank's backend conversation.

◒
Think of this as the story's production notebook.

Director tells the AI how to play the scene. Continuity preserves important facts. Story Tools preserve structure and history around the scene.

Restore means Scene State — not every recorded setting.

The snapshot also records its chapter, Director preset, active Guard Rails and enabled Continuity for the copied alternate-scene package/reference. Restore Scene State reapplies the saved Scene State only; it does not automatically re-enable those other systems or rewind Clank messages.

Package the Furina side of the story.

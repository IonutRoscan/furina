WHAT FURINA IS — AND ISN'T

Powerful tools. No pretend magic.

Furina remains a client-side browser extension. It does not modify Clank's backend memory or increase a model's real context window.

Its roleplay systems organize information locally and, when necessary, prepare user-controlled context for the messages you choose to send.

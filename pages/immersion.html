☁ IMMERSION

Give the conversation an atmosphere.

Furina can layer visual ambience, a soundtrack, decorative stickers and distraction-free reading modes around an individual conversation.

None of these are required. They are there when a scene deserves a little more theatre.

✦
These tools are presentation, not AI context.

Rain, music, stickers, Reader Mode and Focus Mode change your local experience. They do not become story facts for the model unless you separately describe those facts in the roleplay.

MUSIC / AMBIENCE

Give one chat its own soundtrack.

Furina accepts direct audio links, SoundCloud links and YouTube links. Playback starts only after user interaction.

01
Load first

Name the track if you want, paste a URL, then load it.

02
Press Play

Browser autoplay rules mean Furina waits for an explicit user action.

03
Embedded media differs

SoundCloud or YouTube may use the controls inside their embedded player for playback and related controls.

♫
A soundtrack is saved for the conversation.

That makes it easy to give different roleplays different ambience instead of changing one global player every time.

READER & FOCUS

Sometimes the best effect is less UI.

Reader Mode creates a cleaner long-form transcript. Focus Mode goes further and temporarily hides Clank and Furina UI.

05
PUTTING IT TOGETHER

Build a mood, not a checklist.

A few coordinated choices usually feel better than every effect enabled at once.

🌧QUIET MYSTERYRainy Night + low-volume ambience

Dark image background, restrained rain, quiet music and Reader Mode for slower scenes.

✨MAGICAL SCENEArcane Glow + character wallpaper

Sparkles, a subtle grade, a few decorative stickers and a character-specific soundtrack.

📺ANALOG UNEASECRT + Old Film styling

Scanlines or grain, restrained saturation and a fitting ambient track without overwhelming the text.

✓
You can turn all of this off instantly.

Immersion should make the roleplay nicer to inhabit. If it starts competing with the writing, simplify it.

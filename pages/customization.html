✦ CUSTOMIZATION

Make Clank feel like yours.

Furina can change much more than the color of a chat. A conversation can have its own background, atmosphere, typography, message style, music and visual identity.

You can keep things simple with a preset or use Full Atelier to tune individual pieces yourself.

✦
You don't need to customize everything.

A theme and background are usually enough to make a chat feel completely different. Everything else is optional.

⚗

LIVE WALLPAPERS

Make the background move.

Live wallpapers use video instead of a static image while keeping Furina's background controls.

LIVE
BACKGROUND EFFECTS Blur · Color grading · Parallax

Treat it like a wallpaper, not a video player.

Furina places the live background behind the chat and lets the conversation remain fully usable.

  • Background darkness
  • Blur
  • Brightness and contrast
  • Saturation and hue
  • Parallax
  • Overlay and vignette
✦
Tip for live wallpapers

Short seamless videos usually work best. If the first and last frames match naturally, the loop will feel much less noticeable.

06
POWER-USER EXTRAS

Polish the experience around the experience.

Full Atelier also exposes a few settings that sit outside ordinary theme editing.

Entrance Animations

Enable or disable Furina's entrance presentation and preview its longer premiere or shorter returning-user animation from the settings area.

Language

Choose Furina's interface language or use Automatic. This changes Furina labels; it does not translate chats, saved notes, presets or personal content.

Custom CSS

Advanced users can add conversation-scoped CSS with optional live preview, then save, disable or reset it.

Diagnostics & Reset

Advanced tools also contain diagnostics and reset controls for troubleshooting a local Furina setup.

Custom CSS is intentionally advanced.

↗ SHARING & PORTABILITY

Back up a layer. Or package the whole experience.

Furina has multiple export systems because a visual theme, a full conversation setup and a roleplay Story Bible are not the same kind of data.

Exports are versioned Furina data. Imports are validated before use, and the visual setup import flow can preview selected components before you choose to keep them.

01
THEME JSON

Share the look without the rest.

Theme export is for Furina's visual theme settings. It does not need to include stickers, music, Director data or your story.

✦FURINA THEMEPurple Butterflies

Versioned visual settings

ColorsBackgroundMessage stylingTypographyLayoutVisual effects
✓
Theme imports preview before you keep them.

Furina can temporarily apply a validated imported theme, then either keep it in the current Theme Scope or restore what you had before.

02
FULL SETUP

Share the atmosphere around a conversation.

A Full Setup combines the visual theme with other immersion settings that make a conversation feel like itself.

FULL SETUP5 components selected
Theme · Reader · Stickers · Atmosphere · Music / Ambience

Nothing changes until you choose what to keep.

◆
Full Setup is not a Story Bible.

It carries the conversation's presentation setup. Director, Continuity and story records use Story Bible portability instead.

03
STORY BIBLE

Move the roleplay's Furina-side story state.

Story Bible JSON can carry multiple story systems together. A Markdown export is also available for a human-readable record.

◆Continuity

Vault entries and continuity settings.

🎬Director

Persistent notes plus advanced Director tools.

◒Scene State

≡Scene Intelligence

★Bookmarks

▦Chapters

04
MIYABI PROFILE DATA

Profile customization has its own backup.

Profile Atelier can export and import Miyabi settings separately from chat themes and story packages.

◎Why separate?

Profile layout settings target your connected profile rather than a chat conversation.

↗Export

Keep a portable copy of your profile appearance and effects.

↙Import

Restore a previous Miyabi setup.

↺Reset

Reset Miyabi without treating it as a chat-theme reset.

05
IMPORT SAFETY

Preview first. Keep backups. Know the scope.

01Use Furina-formatted files

Version and format checks protect against treating arbitrary JSON as a Furina export.

02Check Theme Scope

A theme you keep may save globally or to the current conversation depending on the active scope.

03Preview Full Setup components

Full Setup imports can select which available pieces should be previewed and committed.

04Back up important story state

Especially before using Story Bible replace mode or resetting a large configuration.
